# Learning Behaviours contract

Learning Behaviours records non-academic observations such as participation, preparation, self-management, collaboration, and communication. It is independently operational and must never modify evidence, mastery, or academic grades.

Principals and superusers govern versioned frameworks and assign an active framework plus an active scale to a Gradebook. Indicators are grouped by configurable domain rather than fixed workbook columns.

An observation belongs to one student, Gradebook, indicator, scale level, observer, and date. Observations are immutable. Corrections create a replacement linked to the original with a mandatory reason. Period summaries are projections over current observations and remain separate from academic results.
