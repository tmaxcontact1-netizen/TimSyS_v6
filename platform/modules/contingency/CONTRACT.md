# Contingency contract

Contingency defines what should happen when a planned condition fails or a trigger occurs. It owns triggers, sequenced fallback actions, responsibility references, deadlines, resource requirements, activation evidence and resolution evidence. Resource links are neutral references; inventory, rooms, transport and communications retain their own data and execution authority. Activation requires at least one fallback action. Resolution requires every active action to be completed, skipped or withdrawn and a resolution summary. Resolved plans are immutable.
