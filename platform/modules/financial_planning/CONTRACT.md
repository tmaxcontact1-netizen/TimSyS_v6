# Financial planning contract

Financial planning is an operational budgeting component, not a general ledger. All money is stored in integer minor units and an explicit ISO-style three-letter currency. It owns budgets, estimates, commitments, expenses, credits and expenditure requests. Approvals and documents remain external authorities referenced by component and ID. Approved budget limits are immutable unless returned to draft. Paid requests require approval; reconciliation requires evidence and notes. Closed budgets and reconciled requests are immutable.
