# Learning Standards component contract

Learning Standards owns versioned standards frameworks, their hierarchy, subject/grade applicability, and assignment to gradebooks. Academic Structure remains authoritative for subjects and Gradebook Provisioning for instances.

Active frameworks are immutable. Revised wording, codes, hierarchy, or applicability creates a new draft version. Historical evidence continues to reference its original standard and framework version. Standards may be mapped many-to-many with assessments; this component never owns assessment evidence or calculates mastery.
