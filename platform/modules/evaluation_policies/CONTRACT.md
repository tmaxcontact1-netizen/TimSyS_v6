# Evaluation Policies component contract

Evaluation Policies owns centrally governed, versioned instructions for turning evidence into results. Policies resolve by gradebook, course/subject, programme, then school. Teachers cannot alter policy definitions. Active versions are immutable; historical results retain their policy version.

Policies explicitly define model, aggregation, category weighting, missing-work treatment, minimum evidence, rounding, output scale, and permitted overrides. Preview is advisory and never changes evidence or published results.
