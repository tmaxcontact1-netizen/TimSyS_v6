# Late Entries contract

Late Entries records arrival facts and proposes attendance reconciliation. Attendance and Classroom Attendance own attendance marks. No proposed change is applied without an authorised human confirmation.

Policies are immutable once activated, versioned and effective-dated. Equivalence calculations preserve their contributing tardies. Unresolved schedule context and incomplete records are retained and surfaced, never skipped.

The component publishes universal platform health and operational insights. Its parts do not publish independent health or insights.
