# Grade Evaluation component contract

Grade Evaluation applies a resolved, versioned Evaluation Policy to immutable Assessment Evidence. It supports points, percentage, traditional, standards, and hybrid results through one explainable engine. Every result records policy version, included evidence, exclusions, completeness, confidence, and calculation explanation.

Missing-state treatment is exclusively policy-driven. Attendance and learning behaviours do not alter academic results. Overrides never replace calculated values: they require permission and a reason, remain visibly distinct, and retain the original calculation.
