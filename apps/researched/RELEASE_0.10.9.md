# Research’Ed 0.10.9 — Ambiguity in validation

Adds separate ambiguity status, documentary explanations and cited alternative interpretations to initial and final human validation judgements. Alternatives never count as supported findings; Explicit and Contextual remain equal. Historical judgements without an annotation are shown as not assessed.

Filter flagged/unresolved/unassessed passages, compare supported findings with and without unresolved passages, and download grouped JSON, HTML and PDF validation reports with provenance and decision history. Reports clearly cover the selected validation sample, not the whole corpus. Original analysis reports and MCF v1.0 remain unchanged.

349 regression tests, TypeScript/production build, and isolated database/browser/export acceptance checks. No live corpus analysis, classifier changes, database migration or shared component changes. Research’Ed-only update; published non-Research’Ed bundles retained unchanged.
