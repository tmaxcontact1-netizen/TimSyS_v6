Established strategy benchmarks

Each benchmark receives its own simulated 10 SOL account and sees the same candidates, executable quotes, costs and non-negotiable safety gates. It cannot consume the TimSyS profiles’ funds. This makes the comparison meaningful: a positive result shows what the published rule achieved under the same conditions, not what it might have achieved in theory.
