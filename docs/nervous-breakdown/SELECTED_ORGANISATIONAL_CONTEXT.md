# Selected Organisational Context

Selection answers what a responsibility connects to, where it hands off, and where the mapped evidence stops. It is a deterministic, read-only Layer 1 projection, not a recommendation or workflow.

`selected-context.mjs` derives current direct organisational relationships (with recorded incoming/outgoing/symmetric direction), documentary companions, statement-family membership and unresolved handoffs from the permission-filtered topology. Handoffs are indexed by `responsibility_id`. The existing model has an originating responsibility and a target role, but no receiving responsibility: it cannot describe an incoming unresolved handoff at a responsibility or traverse beyond that endpoint. No endpoint is manufactured.

All renderers, the selection summary and inspector consume the same context. Selection through nodes, callouts, search, List and inspector links shares one action. An exact unique ID selects and locates directly; ambiguous wording retains a result list. Filters remain selected. Summary facts describe the current governed topology; evidence/type/direction controls still constrain the exploratory trail. A current relationship in the summary may therefore be absent from a filtered trail.

`selectGraph` retains its existing base-filter contract. Explore explicitly requests the selected projection, which retains the selected responsibility and immediate eligible relationship endpoints across discovery filters. Its directly attached handoffs appear independently of the global overlay toggle. Global handoffs remain optional and are counted separately. Explicit family expansion retains all permission-eligible members without changing classifications or the role/domain controls. The primary filter count is distinct from retained context and total corpus scope.

Focus uses the shared context. Direct organisational endpoints take priority; the selected documentary family is secondary until Show documentary family is requested. The existing traversal result—including REL-010 symmetric zero-hop group closure—is unchanged. Focus presentation does not change graph mathematics, source identities, evidence, review states or global semantic coordinates. The selected responsibility stays at its original anchor. The generic title is **Focused organisational context**, to avoid implying a process sequence.

Selection exposes the responsibility headline, role/domain/level, each direct relationship and each handoff separately, evidence/review state, source access and secondary family action. The detailed handoff inspector retains its original rationale, role-mapping distinction, source/version trace and history. Unresolved endpoint cards identify the known role and unknown receiving responsibility, with inspect actions. They are overlays, never responsibility nodes. Documentary evidence for a handoff remains documentary even when the endpoint is unresolved.

3D and accelerated 2D use clickable keyboard-accessible callout buttons, priority-based collision suppression, leader lines and screen-space node hit tolerance. Selected records have a textual SELECTED marker, border and halo. Direct endpoints reserve annotation space ahead of companions and background. The Canvas fallback offers equivalent callouts; List uses the shared summary and selectable rows. Hidden annotations never remove graph records. Controls and the full-width inspector-below layout remain intact.
